Hod

One storage system that keeps every Sims 1 file byte-perfect forever, and the tools that read and write it: a browser you can open anything in, a save editor that can't lose your game, and eventually a community exchange. A hod carries bricks so you can build; hodós is Greek for path. Both fit.

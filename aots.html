Attack of the Show

G4's daily show ran from March 2005 to January 2013 and was never properly indexed. Most of it isn't streaming anywhere. I'm working through what survives and writing down what actually happened in each episode — who turned up, what they were there for, and where in the runtime to find it.

How these are made

Each episode gets transcribed, then read through and broken down into hosts, appearances, segments and topics. Only the finished notes end up on this site — no transcripts, no video, nothing that isn't mine to publish. Timestamps point into the episode, not to a copy of it.
